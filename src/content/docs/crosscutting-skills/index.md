Crosscutting skills for efficient and reliable work with LLMs and agents:

- **[Engineering](engineering/):** Use coding agents to increase productivity, standardize workflows, and delegate tasks.
- **[Evaluation](evaluation/):** Assess the quality, reliability, and safety of AI systems through systematic evaluations.

Crosscutting skills are iterative: each skill progresses through three levels with its own outcomes, and each level extends and enhances the one before it.
