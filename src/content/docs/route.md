The route has three milestones. Each milestone covers one area and the matching level of each practice.

Follow the milestones in order, keeping earlier ones in use. At every milestone, choose the simplest tool that fits the task.

## Approach

Every milestone follows the same approach:

1. Learn the area topics to gain a broad understanding.
2. Develop the practice capabilities in daily work with agents.
3. Deploy the area infrastructure in local or self-hosted setups.
4. Complete the area exercises to consolidate the milestone.

## Milestones

### Guide

Build with agents interactively, guiding each step and checking each output.

- Area: [LLM applications](../areas/llm-applications/)
- Engineering: [Personal productivity](../practices/engineering/#personal-productivity)
- Evaluation: [Prompt outputs](../practices/evaluation/#prompt-outputs)

### Scale

Extend what agents can do, share it with the team, and give agents feedback on their own output.

- Area: [Agent capabilities](../areas/agent-capabilities/)
- Engineering: [Team productivity](../practices/engineering/#team-productivity)
- Evaluation: [Workflow outcomes](../practices/evaluation/#workflow-outcomes)

### Delegate

Run agents unattended, monitor and score their work rather than guide it, and coordinate agents on larger tasks.

- Area: [Agentic systems](../areas/agentic-systems/)
- Engineering: [Task delegation](../practices/engineering/#task-delegation)
- Evaluation: [Agent behaviors](../practices/evaluation/#agent-behaviors)
