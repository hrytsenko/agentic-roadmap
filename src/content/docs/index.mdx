- **Mainline skills:** Skills for applying AI in practice, each adding a new area, from LLM applications to complete agentic systems that enable new business capabilities.
- **Crosscutting skills:** Skills that operate across the full roadmap rather than within a single mainline skill, accelerating delivery and maintaining the quality and reliability of outcomes.
